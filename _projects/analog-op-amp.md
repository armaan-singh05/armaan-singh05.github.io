## Overview

For ENSC 325 I designed a CMOS folded-cascode op-amp, taking it from schematic capture to simulated
performance checked against the design requirements.

## What I did

- Captured the schematic and ran electrical simulations in Cadence OrCAD.
- Organized schematic libraries and wrote engineering documentation to support validation.
- Checked circuit performance against the requirements to confirm it operated reliably.
